# Catch-up walk, Nov 19–25 (48 games)

A retrospective, day by day, done on 2026-11-26. For each day I read only what was published before it, wrote a probability for every game into a log, and only then fetched that day's results, box scores and recaps. I learned from them and moved to the next day.

**It isn't ledger evidence.** None of these games were recorded before tip (the daily record last ran on Nov 18), so the season ledger, the pitch tickets and morning-1 have no rows for these days. They are never backfilled.

## How the numbers were made
- **Model:** the leak-free walk-forward replay (`replay-season.js`, spec replay-9, engine 2026-11-07.rotcap).
  - It uses only box scores before each day and the latest official injury report at least 60 minutes before each tip.
  - Its leak checks pass: future-blind and scrambled-future runs are identical, and the control changes predictions as it should.
- **Market:** DraftKings' close from ESPN's odds service.
  - 6 of 6 sampled games match ESPN's core endpoint exactly.
  - On Nov 18, all 12 closes match the live ledger.
- **Me:** the close as a starting point, moved only where my read from the earlier days' box scores and recaps disagreed. Each pick was written with its reason before the day's results were fetched.
- **Results:** ESPN scoreboard and box score agree on all 48 games.

**The main lesson is about evidence, not basketball.** Every pattern I picked up from one to three games either reversed or stayed too small to judge within days. My adjustments were noise around the close. This matches the earlier research: the situational scans (situ-1) and absence-1 found nothing beyond the close, and narratives have no edge either. What beat the close this week was the plain model, and the season record says even that is a hot week rather than a proven edge.
